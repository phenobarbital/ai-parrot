**Query**: W001 + wiki pages (TASK-2205, mem-8b736514d2dc, api/tenant.py) · **Confidence**: high

Since the brainstorm, formdesigner tenancy was redesigned (FEAT-421 "Client-declared tenant in the forms URL"):
- PR #1146 (`request['tenant_context']`) and #1149 (middleware in `app.py`) were **rejected**; TASK-2205 reverts any middleware from app.py. Recorded rationale (decision memory mem-8b736514d2dc): a middleware in `app.py` ships to no wheel client, and `setup_form_api` mounts on the ROOT router so no middleware can be forms-scoped. "Middleware is structurally wrong here."
- Replacement shipped: `parrot_formdesigner/api/tenant.py` — per-route `requires_tenant()` decorator composed into `_wrap_auth` (api/routes.py:69), tenant declared in URL (`/api/v1/t/{tenant}/...`), `declared_tenant(request)`, `enforce_membership_unless_public`, `assert_body_tenant_matches` (400 on body/URL mismatch). Authorization against session `programs` + superuser flag.
- IMPACT: brainstorm §1 (`parrot.saas.middleware.tenant_middleware` registered in app.py) contradicts this recorded decision's *reasoning*. Differences that may still justify middleware for SaaS: the SaaS surface would live in its OWN subapp/app assembly owned by `ai-parrot-saas` (not shipped-to-arbitrary-hosts), and API-key M2M auth has no navigator-auth session to decorate around. Needs a human decision (→U1). Also: brainstorm's `_get_request_tenant`/`programs[0]` references describe the OLD formdesigner heuristic — the new canonical primitive to generalize is `tenant.py`, not `_utils.py`.
